How to read him

You do not need to know any medicine. You need to read four numbers.

NumberNormalWhat it means when it is off
RRbreathing12–20 The one that matters most. Over 25 means genuinely sick. It moves before anything else does.
HRpulse60–100 Over 120 means the body is compensating hard.
BPtop number110–140 Under 100 in someone young means compensation has already failed. Late, and bad.
SpO₂oxygen95–100 Under 92 means oxygen is not getting in.

Two rules that cover most of it

  • Pulse up and pressure down is shock. Look at the skin first. Always.
  • Breathing fast but oxygen normal means it is not the lungs. Something else is driving it — look at cognition and hydration.

Which examination to reach for

Shockedskin, general

Belly painabdominal, hydration

Two more things. They are all hiding something, and they only let go of it the second time you ask — one question is never enough. And his pulse reacts before his mouth does.

The thresholds are the ones the NHS uses at the bedside (NEWS2).
